# Benchmark Report Template

Sections:

- Metrics with nullable values, explicit numerators/denominators, applicability,
  and 95% Wilson intervals for direct proportions
- Denominator definitions and undefined reasons
- Mapping and quality-score disagreements with machine-readable reason codes
- Portable input/output provenance from the deterministic benchmark manifest
- Benchmark scope and human-review boundary

Mapping agreement uses exact study, source-variable, common-variable, and
review-status tuples.
